Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Applies to the SMS Relay Android app (package com.saulredemptionservicesllc.smsrelay)

In short: SMS Relay does not collect, upload, sell or share any of your data. It has no internet access. The only thing that leaves your phone is the forwarded SMS, sent by your carrier to the numbers you chose.

Who we are

SMS Relay is published by Saul Redemption Services LLC ("we", "us"). Contact: saulredemptionservicesllc@gmail.com. The app's source code is public at github.com/ajay-develops/sms-relay, so anyone can check these statements.

What the app does with your messages

When a text message arrives, SMS Relay reads its sender, text and the SIM it arrived on, and compares them with the forwarding rules you created. If a rule matches, the app sends a copy of the message as a regular SMS, from your phone, to the recipients in that rule. Messages that don't match any rule are ignored and not stored.

Data we collect

None. The app has no internet permission, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We have no servers that receive information from the app.

Sharing

We don't share data with anyone. Forwarded messages are delivered by your mobile carrier to the recipients you chose, the same as any SMS you send yourself; your carrier's privacy policy applies to that delivery.

Your choices

You can turn off or delete any rule, clear the activity log, revoke permissions in Android settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes all of its data.

SMS Relay is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
