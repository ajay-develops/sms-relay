SMS Relay forwards the text messages you choose to another phone number — automatically, in the background.

<b>Simple rules</b>
• Forward from one number, several numbers, or everyone
• Works with bank and service sender IDs like "HDFCBK"
• Send to one or more numbers
• Pick senders and recipients from your contacts, or type them in

<b>Made for dual-SIM phones</b>
• Only forward messages that arrive on SIM 1, SIM 2, or both
• Choose which SIM sends the forward

<b>Reliable</b>
• Keeps working when the app is closed
• Long messages are forwarded in full
• Failed sends retry automatically when signal returns
• Recent activity shows what was forwarded and whether it was delivered
• Built-in loop protection: never forwards back to the sender, and ignores its own forwards

<b>Private by design</b>
• No internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics
• Your rules and activity stay on your phone
• Free and open source (GPL-3.0)

Forwarded messages are sent from your SIM as normal SMS, so your carrier's usual rates apply.
